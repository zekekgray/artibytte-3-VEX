What VEX does.

Four capabilities, reused across every product in the ecosystem.

Document & Data Extraction

Feed it invoices, receipts, supplier files or a photo of a dashboard — VEX pulls out clean, structured data automatically.

Workflow Automation

Rule-based automation that watches for triggers — an overdue payment, a low-stock item, a missed check-in — and acts without a person in the loop.

AI Model Integration

One abstraction layer connects Gemini, Grok and OpenAI into every product, so each system gets the right model for the job.

Custom Reporting

Reports generated on demand, pulled from whatever data a product already has — no separate export-and-clean step.

Attention Flags

Surfaces what actually needs a look — no-shows, overdue accounts, shortages — instead of a dashboard someone has to check.

One Engine, Every Product

Built once, reused everywhere — every new ArtiBytte product launches on the same proven extraction and automation layer.
